A private video downloader you run yourself.

Magpie saves the stream behind any player as one clean MP4. There is no company in the middle. You install it, it runs in your browser, and what you watch stays on your machine.

Install

Takes about a minute. The Firefox build is signed by Mozilla.

Chromium based browsers

Chrome, Edge, Brave, or any browser built on Chromium 116 or newer.
Download zip
  1. Unzip the download to a folder you will keep.
  2. Open chrome://extensions. In Edge it is edge://extensions, in Brave brave://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode. It is in the top right, or the left sidebar in Edge.
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  5. Pin Magpie from the extensions menu in the toolbar.

Firefox

Firefox 140 or newer. Stays installed across restarts.
Install for Firefox
  1. Click the button above in Firefox.
  2. Click Continue to Installation, then Add.
  3. Pin Magpie from the extensions menu in the toolbar.
In another browser the button saves the file. Drag it onto a Firefox window to install. Updates do not install themselves; install a newer file over the old one.

How it works

Four clicks, one file.

01

Press play

Open a page with a video and start it.

02

Open the popup

The badge counts the streams Magpie found on this tab. Each one shows a frame of itself.

03

Pick a quality

Click a stream, choose a rendition, hit Download.

04

Done

One MP4 in your downloads folder. History remembers it, and Magpie hides media you already have.

Inside the popup

Three tabs and a settings panel. Nothing else to open.

This page. Every stream the tab is playing, with a frame of each. Media you already have is hidden, and media that is downloading goes gray.

Downloads. Running, queued and failed, with speed and time left. Finished ones leave the list the moment they are saved.

History. Title, date, length and the page it came from. Search it, or clear it.

A frame of every stream

The picture comes from the stream itself, not the page: one segment, decoded in your browser, kept as a few kilobytes. Off in Settings if you prefer.

A queue that fits your connection

Parallel downloads is a setting, two by default. Extra clicks wait in line and start on their own as slots free up.

Live recording

Live streams and webcams get a record button. The recording runs until you press Stop or the stream ends.

Knows what you already have

Media in your history is hidden from This page. Media that is downloading goes gray, and clicking it shows the job instead of starting it twice.

Private windows stay private

Downloads from a private window are forgotten when the last private window closes, unless you choose to keep them.

Your session rides along

Sites that check cookies or the Referer work, because the segments are fetched by your browser with your login, from the site already serving them.

Private by design

Most video downloaders pay for themselves with your attention or your data. This one has nothing to sell.

No ads, no upsells

No banners, no sponsored links, no "premium" tier, no nag screens. Every feature is in the free download because there is only the free download.

No tracking, no telemetry

No analytics, no crash reports, no usage pings. The list of what you saved stays in your browser and never leaves it. The only requests Magpie makes are for the video you asked for, sent to the site already serving it.

No account, no server

Nothing to sign up for and no cloud converter. Segments are fetched, decrypted and stitched inside your browser, then handed straight to your downloads folder.

You run it yourself

The whole source is on GitHub. Read it, build it from scratch, or load the zip from this page. No store listing, no auto updates you did not ask for.

Check it yourself. Inspect Magpie from chrome://extensions or about:debugging and watch the Network tab while you download. You will see requests to the video's own site and nothing else. This website follows the same rule: no analytics, no cookies, no third party scripts or fonts.
